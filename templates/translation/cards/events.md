# Blank translation worksheet: events

Follow the [current Russian events](../../../game/ru/cards/events.md), [shared inventory](../../../game/deck.md) and [old-to-new number table](../../../game/event-numbering.md). EV-001–EV-331 are physical card numbers: 30 Coffee copies, 299 other ordinary events, WAKE UP and the custom blank. Preserve every numbered row; each has one physical copy. Titles and effects stay empty for a new translation. EV-331 has a custom blank effect.

Historical notes use a different numbering system. This worksheet uses current numbers; the [previous worksheet](https://github.com/NEO-Wake-UP/wake-up-card-game/blob/ae5afb42e89d9c16a2f776c86db843dbbcb55433/templates/translation/cards/events.md) retains the earlier IDs.

Follow the [wording and coin-face notation approved on 28 October](../../../design/reviews/2026-10-28-wording-and-coin-faces.ru.md). Coin faces in effects are written (W) and (UP); the title WAKE UP stays unchanged. “You and a chosen player” names two distinct participants; “you and two chosen players” names three.

See the [final wording polish approved on 28 October](../../../design/reviews/2026-10-28-final-wording-polish.ru.md). Equivalent English wording is retained where it already matches the approved Russian meaning.

Follow the [status balance and clarity changes approved on 28 October](../../../design/reviews/2026-10-28-status-balance-and-child-clarity.ru.md). Collector now receives normal salary; Minimalist has a separate 200B month-end bonus.
