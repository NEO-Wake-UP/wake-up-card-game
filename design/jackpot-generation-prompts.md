выигрыш из Банка

удваивает его.

Festive accents are secondary to the gameplay.
Avoid: dark text panels, glowing body letters, visual noise behind type, tiny type, extra text, photorealism, a photo of a card, external background, hands, multiple cards, changing the gameplay.
