4.1. The cap applies to paid distribution of original, translated and modified physical editions in reliance on this licence. The permitted price per copy is:

- **C** is the documented manufacturing cost per completed copy of the particular print run.
- **T(P)** is the tax legally payable in connection with the sale of that copy at its actual price P, taking account of the applicable regime, reliefs and credits, insofar as not included in C.

Do not add taxes on top of the resulting cap a second time. This limits markup on C + T; it does not authorise a 50% profit margin on revenue. Lower prices and free distribution are allowed; there is no minimum price.

4.2. Calculate C by dividing justified manufacturing costs of the run by the number of usable copies manufactured, not merely those sold. Include materials, all components and boxes, printing and technical prepress for the run, assembly, production labour and mandatory associated charges, and a justified allocation of directly related manufacturing overhead. Deduct discounts and refunds; exclude recoverable taxes. Count each expense once only.

Creating a new game, illustrations or translation, advertising, selling expenses, platform fees, trade storage, administration, financing, penalties and publisher/intermediary profit do not increase C; they must be funded from the permitted markup. Genuine payments to independent manufacturers on ordinary market terms may be included in C. Related-party trading markups, fictitious services and artificially inflated expenses intended to evade the cap may not. A publisher's wholesale selling price for a finished run does not become a new manufacturing cost for an intermediary.

4.3. T includes only documented taxes attributable to that copy's sale, without counting the same amount in C, another tax or another distribution stage. Use actual tax bases and rates; an arbitrary “tax surcharge” is not permitted. Where tax depends on price or profit, account for that dependency when checking the inequality rather than substituting a discretionary fixed addition.
